# Code of Conduct

[Português (Brasil)](pt-BR/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

USB Presence Gate welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Project discussions should be safe, respectful and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence and decisions rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and accept respectful disagreement.
- Be honest about authorship, test results, limitations and mistakes.
- Respect other contributors' time, privacy and boundaries.
- Credit prior work and respect its license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, publication of private information without permission, impersonation and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to participation in this repository, including issues, pull requests, reviews and project-related interactions. They do not impose additional restrictions on independent use or modification of the software under the MIT license.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private contact channel if one is available. If none is available, a public issue may request a private contact method without including sensitive details, names of affected people or evidence that exposes private information. For violations of GitHub's platform rules, GitHub's own reporting tools are also available.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or report violations to GitHub, depending on the circumstances. Reports should be handled discreetly where possible. No response time or confidentiality guarantee is promised.
